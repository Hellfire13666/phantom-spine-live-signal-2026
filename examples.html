EASY · INDEPENDENT LIVE ENGINE

1. example.com through real DNS

Queries fixed HTTPS endpoints for Google and Cloudflare DNS. Displays actual Google A/AAAA/MX/NS and Cloudflare A/AAAA results, plus exact public source links. Works without any companion tools.

"$HOME/.local/bin/phantom-spine-live-v020" hunt example.com --engine native --providers dns --timeout 5

The terminal will display [OK] or [PARTIAL] for each provider request, a unique observation count, and [SAVE OFF]. No specific DNS answers are promised in advance.

Real public-source observations, not direct requests to facebook.com.
